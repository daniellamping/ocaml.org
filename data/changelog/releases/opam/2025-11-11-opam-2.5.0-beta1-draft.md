<p>This is the first beta release of opam 2.5.0.</p>
<p>Binaries and full archive are signed by the <a href="https://opam.ocaml.org/opam-dev-pubkey.pgp" rel="nofollow">opam dev team</a> (fingerprint <code>92C5 26AE 50DF 3947 0EB2 911B ED4C F1CA 67CB AA92</code>).</p>
<p>Please see our <a href="https://opam.ocaml.org/blog/opam-2-5-0-beta1" rel="nofollow">blog post</a> for a highlight on the major changes and upgrade instructions.</p>
<p><a href="https://github.com/ocaml/opam/blob/2.5.0-beta1/CHANGES">Changelog</a>:</p>
